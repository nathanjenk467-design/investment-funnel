# Investment Ideas

Run **only after Nathan keeps a thesis**.

Macro writes the ideas. Architect puts them on the local Funnel column **Investment Ideas**. Micro does not write this column.

An idea is the **shape of what we might sit with**. Not a stock name. Not a buy. Not a ticker. The idea card itself is not a Candidates-tab row.

A keep of a thesis is not automatically names. **A new idea landing is.** The moment Architect places an `IDEA-*` on the Funnel, Micro runs [`names.md`](names.md): 2–4 discussable names for that idea. A sit-off idea still gets stay-off names. An honest empty needs a why.

---

## Two passes, in this order

1. **From that thesis alone.** What sit does this one judgement suggest, if we pretend no other live thesis exists.
2. **From that thesis plus each other live thesis.** Every pair that can fight is a **fork / grid**. Write every live branch as its own idea. Do not pick a winner.

If there is only one live thesis, pass 2 is “no pair yet.” Write that.

If there are three live theses, do the new one against each other live one. Do not build a 3-way mash-up that assumes all three stay true. Pairs first. A three-way note is allowed only as a pointer: “these two grids both touch TH-03; we have not collapsed them.”

---

## Pass 1 — from the kept thesis alone

Write one idea. The sit can be **sit with** or **sit off**. Sit off is still an idea that lands. It still triggers names (stay-off names). “No honest sit-shape yet” is an idea only if you write it as sit off with a why; it still lands, and Micro still owes names or an honest empty.

Required:

- **Parent** — the kept `TH-*`
- **The sit** — the shape, in ordinary language
- **What it is not** — the obvious wrong vehicle (the next model, the spender, the famous name everyone already uses as a shortcut)
- **What has to stay true** — inherited from the thesis
- **How this sit can be false even if the thesis is true** — the landlord does not get paid; the surplus stays with the customer; the queue is mostly phantom
- **Clock** — tag, inherited or a written reason it differs
- **Already in the price, or not checked** — still no ticker

Do not write a company. Do not write a ticker. If the sit cannot be said without a company name, the sit is not clear yet. That is Macro’s problem to fix. Micro names the vehicles **after** this idea lands, not in this file.

One honest sit is better than four cousins of the same sit.

---

## Pass 2 — pair fork / grid

When two theses can fight, **that is the point**. Do not write one sit that assumes both stay true.

### The rule

1. Name the two theses.
2. Name the axes they actually fight on. Each axis is a world fork, not a mood.
3. Write **every live branch** as its own `IDEA-*`.
4. Do not pick a winner.
5. Do not bury a branch as a footnote.
6. A branch that feels ugly still gets a card if it is possible.

If a branch is not possible, write why it is not possible and omit the card. “We do not like it” is not “not possible.”

### The 15 August example (method only)

Not a dump of the live board. Not a keep in this document. The session’s pair:

- **TH-01** — they keep building; the plants stay scarce.
- **TH-02** — the circular funding of that build will not hold.

Two axes the pair actually opens:

| | Paper holds | Paper breaks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Factory gets paid** | Branch A | Branch B |
| **Factory does not get paid** | Branch C | Branch D |

All four are possible.

- **A — factory paid, paper holds.** They keep building. Scarcity stays. The circular paper does not break. This is the sit people write if they quietly assume both theses stay comfortable. It is a real branch. Write it. Do not treat it as “the” sit.
- **B — factory paid, paper breaks.** The paper blows in public and the plants still collect. Possible. Write it. Do not assume a paper break starves the buildings.
- **C — factory unpaid, paper holds.** The paper still looks fine. The factories are not collecting. “What if the factories don’t get paid” is a real branch, not a footnote.
- **D — factory unpaid, paper breaks.** A blow-up that starves the buildings. This forces a look at TH-01. Possible. Write it. Do not fold TH-01 in advance.

They can both be right (A, and also the tension note on the thesis cards). They can also meet in D. The grid holds all four. Synthesis is the grid, not a winner.

Write four ideas, each naming its branch. Each idea says which thesis it is sitting with **in this branch**, and which thesis it is not relying on.

### What each pair-idea writes

- **Parents** — the two `TH-*` ids
- **Branch** — which cell of the grid, in words
- **The sit for this branch only**
- **What it is not** — including “not the sit that assumes the other branch”
- **What has to stay true on this branch**
- **What would make this branch false**
- **What this branch does to the other thesis** — leave it, force a look, or sit in tension (they may or may not contradict; we think ____)
- **Already in the price, or not checked**

No ticker on the idea card. No buy. Names wait for the idea to land, then [`names.md`](names.md).

---

## What this workflow must not do

- Do not pick a winner so the column looks decisive.
- Do not write one sit that needs both theses to stay true unless that sit is **Branch A** (or the equivalent “both hold” cell) and is labeled as that cell.
- Do not skip the unpaid-factory branch because it is awkward.
- Do not skip the “factory paid and paper never breaks” branch because it sounds like consensus.
- Do not write `ST-*` rows in this file. Macro does not name. Micro names after the idea lands.
- Do not hand Micro a ticker “for example” inside the idea card.
- Do not skip calling Micro after an idea lands. The trigger is the landing, not Nathan asking.
- Do not use the old “thesis candidate” label.

---

## After the ideas are written

Architect places the `IDEA-*` cards on the local Investment Ideas column.

That placement **is** the trigger. Architect tells Micro. Micro runs [`names.md`](names.md) for each new idea: 2–4 discussable names, stay-off names if the idea is sit off, or an honest empty with a why.

Macro writes the notebook: ideas offered, in one line each; Nathan has not been asked to pick a branch.

Macro does not refine the ideas until the world moves ([`refine.md`](refine.md) is for kept theses first; an idea is refreshed when its parent thesis is refined).
