# Refine a kept thesis

Run when the world moves against a live thesis, and as Macro’s standing ownership after a keep.

Macro owns the card. Architect updates the local board. Nathan is not asked to re-keep a wording polish.

---

## What Macro may change

- **Board-measure** — a new print strengthens, weakens, is a different flavor, or is left alone.
- **Tension notes** — refresh against every live thesis. A new keep elsewhere can change the note.
- **What would make this false / what is not a reason to fold** — only if a new world print sharpened them.
- **Already in the price, or not checked** — may move from “not checked” to a dated read, or back to “not checked” if the old read is stale.
- **Evidence line** — an agreeing Board item sits here. It is not a new thesis.

## What Macro may not change

- **The judgement itself**, unless the world forces it.
- The live `TH-*` id.
- History. Do not edit the old claim so the old call disappears.

If the world forces a new call, write a **new draft** ([`thesis-daily.md`](thesis-daily.md)) and offer it. The old thesis stays live until Nathan folds it. Macro does not fold. Macro does not mint the replacement as live.

“The wording got cleaner” is not the world forcing a rewrite.

---

## When the world has moved

A world move is a dated fact on the Board, or a short grab that the thesis itself named (the print that would make it false, or the print that would strengthen it).

A share-price scare, by itself, is not a world move. It may update “already in the price.” It does not rewrite the judgement. The 15 August pattern: a scare in the shares while the plants did not move is a price event, not a factory event.

If the named “what would make this false” has printed:

1. Write that it printed, with the date.
2. Tell Architect. Architect tells Nathan.
3. Notebook: the print, and Nathan’s words if he folds or keeps.
4. Do not rescue the thesis with a cousin story.
5. Do not write a new reason to stay in the same Investment Ideas.

If the print did **not** happen, and only the tape wiggled: refine the priced line. Leave the judgement.

---

## Tension again

Every refine pass re-reads the other live theses.

If two theses still sit in tension, keep the note:

> We notice the tension. They may or may not contradict. We think ____.

Do not resolve the fight on the thesis card by picking a winner. The fork lives on Investment Ideas ([`investment-ideas.md`](investment-ideas.md)). If a new branch became possible, or an old branch became impossible, refresh the pair-ideas and say why the branch is no longer possible (a world print, not a preference).

---

## Ideas that sit under this thesis

When you refine a thesis, walk its `IDEA-*` cards.

- If the thesis is still live, the alone-idea stays unless its own “how this sit can be false” printed.
- If a pair-branch is now impossible, mark that branch closed on the idea card, with the print. Do not delete the history.
- If Nathan later folds the thesis, Architect parks or removes the child ideas on the local board. Macro notes it. Macro does not invent a new parent so the ideas can stay.

---

## What refine is not

- Not a second 8am mint.
- Not a daily tape pack.
- Not a name screen.
- Not a chance to collage leftover stories onto a kept card.
- Not a quiet rewrite of the call.
