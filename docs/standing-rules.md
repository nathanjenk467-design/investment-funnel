## 2. Funnel columns

Three columns on the Funnel tab:

1. **Board** — cooked world picture. Kept situations, open calls, housed stories. Not a live pull.
2. **Theses** — judgements Nathan kept. Clock is a **tag on the card**, not its own column. Tags: `now`, `6–12 months`, `next year`, or a dated window you can stand behind.
3. **Investment Ideas** — the shape of what we might sit with. Not a stock name. Not a buy.

The Candidates tab is names, then screen, then monitor / action / book. A keep of a thesis does **not** put names on that tab.

The old label “Thesis candidates” is retired. It collided with the Candidates tab. Write **Investment Ideas**.

The old pair of thesis columns (“Theses 6–12 months” and “Theses now”) is retired as a split. One Theses column. Clock is a tag.

## 3. What each object is

### Board card

A cooked item on the world picture. True enough to stay on the Board. That is not a thesis.

Most Board cards will never become a thesis. That is a working Board.

A story can sit on the Board forever as a story.

Nearby pictures are not a replacement for a judgement. Two cards that sit next to each other are not one thesis.

### Story

A housed account of what happened, what someone said, or how a situation looks from one seat. No duty to name a clock, a tension, or what would make a call false.

Copying a story into Theses and changing the label is the Narrative-card failure. Do not do it.

### Thesis

A **judgement**: a forward-looking call or opinion.

It is not:

- a mechanism
- a fact
- “how something works”
- a collage of leftover stories
- two situations squeezed into one sentence

It is a call you can be wrong about in public.

A draft uses the same shape. Until Nathan keeps it, it is a draft. It does not get a live `TH-*` id. Macro does not mint live.

### Investment Idea

The shape of what we might sit with, written from a kept thesis, or from a pair of kept theses.

It is not a stock name. It is not a buy. It is not a ticker. The idea card is not a name. When it lands, Micro must write 2–4 discussable names ([`names.md`](names.md)).

### Name

A company or instrument on the Candidates tab, written when a new Investment Idea lands. Micro owns names. Macro owns ideas. A thesis keep is not the trigger.

## 4. Language

Write so a stranger can read the card.

- No “money is easy / money is tight” frame. A dated funding fact already on the Board can be part of a judgement. A slogan cannot.
- No unexplained labels, series names, or ticker soup. Prefer “the factory,” “the paper,” “the rail” on Board, thesis, and idea cards. Names belong on the Candidates tab after an idea lands, not on a thesis or an idea.
- No shop slang on cards or in these run files: do not write *hang*, *dies*, *desk skew*, or *kill*. Say what you mean: “what would make this false,” “no longer live,” “already in the price,” “we have not checked.”
- No manager names on cards. Use the method. Do not cite the person.

## 5. Writing gates (every draft, every idea)

**One judgement.** If you need “and also” more than once, you have two calls. Split or stop.

**Not a collage.** Do not sweep leftover Board stories onto the card to make it look complete.

**Not two situations in one sentence.** If card A would be false on one world print and card B on a different print, they are two judgements or they stay on the Board.

**What would make this false.** A world print, not a share-price wiggle. If you cannot name it, you do not understand the call well enough to offer it.

**What is not a reason to fold.** Write the noise that will try (a second scare in the shares, a podcast, a cousin headline).

**Clock as a tag.** `now`, `6–12 months`, `next year`, or a dated window. Not a column. Not a reason to mint the same judgement twice at two speeds.

**Already in the price, or not checked.** One or the other. A fake “still room” line is a bug. “Not checked” is honest and legal.

**Tension with every live thesis.** On a new draft, write a note against each live thesis: we notice the tension, they may or may not contradict, and we think ____. Do not pretend the new card lives alone.

**Nathan keeps.** Silence is not a keep. A notebook entry is not a keep. A strong draft is not a keep.

**Sit is a real output.** A Board full of cards does not obligate a Theses column full of drafts.

## 6. Failure modes (check these, do not rediscover them)

1. **Copying a Narrative card** and renaming it a thesis.
2. **Minting from a tape.** A scare or a bounce in the shares is a price event. It can sit on a priced line. It is not a new judgement.
3. **Minting from a story.** True, vivid, still a story.
4. **Collage.** Plants plus funding plus a payments rail plus an input story, because they shared a week, is a tour.
5. **Easy-money slogan.** Forcing every card through easy/tight money.
6. **Auto-keep.** Draft moved to live Theses without Nathan.
7. **Minting from the notebook.** A past keep reused as a new live id, or a past drop ignored.
8. **Picking a winner** when two theses can fight. That is what the fork is for.
9. **Spawning names because a thesis was kept.** A keep is not names. The trigger is a new Investment Idea. Skipping names after an idea landed, with no why, is the other half of this failure.
10. **Publishing the local board** or pasting live `board.json` / local `index.html` into this repo.
11. **Rewriting a kept judgement** because the wording got prettier. Refine the notes. The judgement stays until the world forces a rewrite.
12. **Inventing why Nathan folded.** If he did not say why, write that he did not say why.
13. **Resolving a Board fight** the Board still marks as a fight.
14. **A ticker in Macro’s output.** That is a bug.
15. **Macro or Micro speaking to Nathan.** Architect carries the talk.

## 8. What we are not building

Not a trading bot. Not a size. Not an order. Not a screen that pretends to be a buy.

The funnel stops at judgement and sit-shape until a new Investment Idea lands. Then Micro writes discussable names. A name is not a buy.
